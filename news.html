Meet the neon dragon: how our newest art style came together

Our new art direction began as a single paint-over on a Friday afternoon. Within a week the whole team was sketching glowing dragons, and the look quickly became the backbone of the next game's world. We kept the shapes bold, the colours saturated and the lighting dramatic, so that every frame works as a poster.
